Drop your background music file here as:  bg-music.mp3

Important — copyright:
Actual Bollywood film songs are copyrighted, so they can't be bundled
into this template. To keep the site legally safe (and avoid it being
muted/blocked when shared on Instagram/WhatsApp), use one of these instead:

1. A royalty-free instrumental (shehnai / strings) from Pixabay Music,
   YouTube Audio Library, or Incompetech — free for personal use.
2. A short instrumental cover/remix of your favourite song that you
   have the rights to use (many wedding-invite sites use this route).
3. If you really want the original film song, license it through a
   platform like Songfreedom / Lickd, or get the couple's own
   recording/permission.

Once you have an mp3, just rename it to bg-music.mp3 and drop it in
this folder — the site is already wired to play/pause it from the
speaker button in the bottom-right corner.
